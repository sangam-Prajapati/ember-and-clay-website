Our Story

Three generations, one recipe book

How It Started

A 12-seat kitchen on Mall Road

In 2014, Chef Rajesh Prajapati opened Ember & Clay with four tables, one secondhand tandoor, and a recipe book his grandmother had written by hand. The goal was simple: cook the food he grew up eating, the way it was meant to be cooked — over real fire, without shortcuts.

Word spread slowly, then quickly. Today the dining room seats eighty, but the kitchen still runs on the same handwritten recipes, the same wood-fired tandoor, and the same insistence on doing things the slow way.

What We Believe

The values behind every plate

Fire Over Shortcuts

If a dish can be cooked over open flame instead of a stovetop, it is. Smoke is a flavor, not a garnish.

Fresh, Not Frozen

Spices are ground daily, dough is kneaded each morning, and nothing sits in a freezer waiting for an order.

Family Recipes, Unchanged

Every core dish on our menu traces back to a handwritten recipe — we've refined technique, never the flavor.
