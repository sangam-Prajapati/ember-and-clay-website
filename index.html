From the Kitchen

Three things we never compromise on

The Tandoor

A 600°C clay oven, fired with mango wood, gives every kebab its char and smoke before it touches a plate.

The Slow Pot

Our curries simmer for hours in small copper pots — never rushed, never reheated from a bulk batch.

The Daily Dough

Naan and roti are kneaded each morning and pressed to order — never frozen, never pre-made.

Our Story

Started in a 12-seat kitchen, run the same way today

Ember & Clay opened with four tables, one tandoor, and a recipe book passed down through three generations. A decade later, the tables have multiplied — the recipes haven't changed.
